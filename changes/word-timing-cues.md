See and hear word timing boundaries

A compact timing bar fills during each foreground word or timed syllable, marks timed syllable boundaries, goes dark in gaps, and counts down to the next word or syllable. Optional clicks can cue whole-word starts and ends or each timed syllable, following the audio at any playback speed with a separate volume control. Background vocal cues are quieter and duet cues use a distinct higher tone. Configure cues using the clock button beside playback or in Audio settings.
