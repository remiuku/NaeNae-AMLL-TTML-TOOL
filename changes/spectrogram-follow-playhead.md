The spectrogram follows the playhead

**Page** jumps ahead when the playhead leaves the view, **Continuous** keeps it centered; pick one in **Preferences → Spectrogram**. Scrolling or dragging pauses it until you press play or leave it for a moment. In Continuous, `Ctrl`+wheel zooms around the playhead and keeps following. Turn it on or off from the spectrogram toolbar or with `Shift+P`.
